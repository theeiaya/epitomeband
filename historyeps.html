OUR HISTORY

Epitome started performing January of 2025. Originally, there were only five members ; Arianne, Grix, Marla, Myco, and Nicolai. Every performance they committed in was inside their highschool, Imus National High School. Invited in each event, they strived to perform more. Eventually, the five became six, now including Precious as their keyboardist after playing alongside the christmas concert.

Currently, Epitome had done 8 performances inside school and 2 outside gigs -- including their biggest event yet, the SSCAI Fest held at Imus Plaza, May 10, 2026. The band pushes to be the best, living up their name as the EPITOME of music.
